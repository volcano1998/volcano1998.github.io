01 / About

I develop computational tools for understanding complex genomes.

I am a PhD candidate in Biomedical Engineering at Vanderbilt University in the laboratory of Prof. Xin Maizie Zhou. My research focuses on detecting and characterizing genetic variation, especially structural variants and copy-number variation in cancer genomes.

I use linked reads, long reads, and single-cell sequencing to develop algorithms that reveal genomic heterogeneity and support more precise diagnosis and treatment.

Based in Nashville, Tennessee. PhD expected May 2026.
