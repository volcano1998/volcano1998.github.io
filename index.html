About me

I am a PhD candidate in Biomedical Engineering at Vanderbilt University, advised by Prof. Xin Maizie Zhou. I develop computational methods for detecting and characterizing genetic variation in complex genomes.

My research focuses on structural variants and copy-number variation in cancer genomics. I leverage linked-read, long-read, and single-cell sequencing data to create algorithms that improve variant detection and reveal tumor heterogeneity.

I received an M.S. in Data Science from Vanderbilt University in 2021 and a B.S. in Chemistry from Fudan University in 2019. Outside the lab, I enjoy brush painting, sketching, and paper craft.
