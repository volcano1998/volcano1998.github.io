Welcome to my homepage!

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Washington, working with Prof. [Evan E. Eichler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_E._Eichler). Previously, I earned a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering at Vanderbilt University, advised by Prof. [Xin Maizie Zhou](https://maiziezhoulab.github.io/), as well as an M.S. in Data Science from Vanderbilt University and a B.S. in Chemistry from Fudan University.

My research focuses on developing high-performance computational methods to detect and characterize complex genetic variation in human populations. During my Ph.D., I developed the following tools:

These tools leverage linked-read, long-read, and single-cell sequencing data to improve variant detection, reveal tumor heterogeneity, and support more precise diagnosis and treatment.

Outside research, I enjoy brush painting, sketching, and paper craft.

### Research interests

* Computational genomics
* Structural variant and copy-number variation detection
* Long-read, linked-read, and single-cell sequencing
* Cancer genomics
* Machine learning and data visualization
